# First-person arm source

`FPS_ARMS_RIG_1.fbx` is "FPS ARMS RIG 1.fbx", unchanged, from **fps arms (rigged only)** by para.

- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/fps-arms-rigged-only
- Download: `fps arms.7z`, 1.4 MB
- Licence: CC0, as shown on that page. Its author notes the mesh and texture started in MakeHuman.
- Attribution is not required, but credit para and the MakeHuman team if the game has a credits screen.

`lookdev/blender/build_arm.py` turns this into `assets/fps_arm.glb`.
